Shorts Publisher

A personal publishing tool for two short-video channels, History Unhinged and Memories of Deen.

Shorts Publisher posts short educational videos that the channel owner creates to the owner's own social media accounts (YouTube, Instagram and TikTok) at scheduled times.

  • The owner connects each of their own accounts once, using the platform's official login.
  • Each week the owner reviews and approves the schedule of videos, captions and privacy settings.
  • At each approved time, the tool uploads that video to the owner's account through the platform's official API.

The tool is used only by its owner, for the owner's own accounts. It does not access other people's accounts, read private messages, or collect data about viewers.
