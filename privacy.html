Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what information Shorts Publisher ("the app") handles and how. The app is a personal tool operated by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

Information the app uses

  • Account connection data: when the owner connects a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account through that platform's official login, the app receives the account's basic profile information (such as account ID, display name and avatar) and access tokens that allow it to publish on the owner's behalf.
  • Content the owner provides: the videos, captions and scheduling choices the owner creates.

The app does not collect information about viewers, followers or any other users, does not read private messages, and does not use cookies or analytics on this website.

How the information is used

Account connection data is used only to publish the owner's approved videos to the owner's own accounts and to show which account is connected. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in a private cloud environment controlled by the owner. They are never published, shared or included in source code.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Information is sent only to the platform being published to (for example TikTok), as needed to publish the video.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while an account is connected. The owner can disconnect at any time by removing the app's access in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services), after which the stored tokens stop working and are deleted. To request deletion of any data, email the contact address below.

Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by its adult owner.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
